# Assign or Download *With Good Reason*

*With Good Reason: An Introduction to Critical Thinking and Argument Mapping* is a digital textbook by Jonathan Reid Surovell and Zachary Poston. It introduces critical thinking through argument mapping and is designed for use with Argumentation.io.

## Assign the textbook and Argumentation.io

Instructors can assign *With Good Reason* through their college or university bookstore. The available courseware bundle includes the digital textbook and a six-month Argumentation.io redemption code.

Students use the redemption code to activate Argumentation.io access through their own accounts. See [Subscribe or Redeem a Code](./SubscribingRedeemingManaging.html) for redemption instructions.

[View *With Good Reason* on VitalSource](https://www.vitalsource.com/products/with-good-reason-an-introduction-to-critical-jonathan-reid-surovell-v9798989005406) for the information needed to assign the book through a college or university bookstore.

## Download the textbook

Downloading *With Good Reason* through Argumentation.io requires one of the following:

- An individual Argumentation.io subscription
- Access through an institutional subscription
- A redeemed Argumentation.io code

Once you have access:

1. Open **Main Menu**.
2. Select **Textbook**.
3. Choose where to save the PDF on your device.

The downloaded PDF can be opened with a compatible PDF reader.
