# Selecting Boxes and Navigating Your Map

You can select any box by clicking on it with the mouse.

Alternatively, you can select the box above, below, to the right of, or to the left of your current selection by using the key commands Shift+Alt+↑, Shift+Alt+↓, Shift+Alt+→, and Shift+Alt+←. 

Other buttons on your keyboard, the Tab, will change what you have selected. However, we strongly recommend using Argumentation’s commands of the form Shift+Alt+arrow key when you’re working on your map. The reason is that these commands give you a greater ability to navigate your map in a logical order.
