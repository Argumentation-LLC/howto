# Navigate and Adjust Your View

You can change which part of a map is visible without changing the map itself. Pan across the canvas, zoom in or out, fit the complete map into the available space, or use the mini-map for an overview.

The pages in this section provide information on:

- [Panning and zooming the map](./adjusting_view.html)
- [Using the View Panel](./view_panel.html)

To move the selection from one box to another with a keyboard, see [Select Boxes and Navigate a Map](./selecting_boxes_and_navigating.html).

The View Panel changes the view of the map canvas. It does not change the map's structure or the size of the Argumentation.io interface. Use your browser's zoom feature when you need to enlarge the interface itself.
