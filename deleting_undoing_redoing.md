# Deleting, Undoing, and Redoing

## Delete a box or branch

1. Select the box you want to delete.
2. Select **Delete** in the toolbar. On Windows, you can instead press `Shift+Alt+D`; on Mac, press `Shift+Option+D`.

Deleting a box also deletes any boxes beneath it. If you delete the main contention of a tree, the entire tree is deleted. A main contention cannot be deleted when it is the only main contention on the canvas.

## Undo or redo your own changes

- To undo, select **Undo**. On Windows, you can instead press `Ctrl+Z`.
- To redo, select **Redo**. On Windows, you can instead press `Ctrl+Y`.

During real-time collaboration, undo and redo apply only to your own changes. You cannot undo or redo another collaborator's changes.
