# Join a Class

Your instructor provides a class-enrollment link. You need an Argumentation.io account to join the class.

1. Sign in to your Argumentation.io account.
2. Open the class-enrollment link supplied by your instructor.
3. Choose to join the class when prompted.
4. Enter the name you want your instructor to see when grading your work.
5. Confirm your choice.

Argumentation.io displays a confirmation that you have joined the class. When the class has assignments available, **My Assignments** appears in **Main Menu**.
